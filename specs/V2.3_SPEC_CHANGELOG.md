## 2. Itemized Changelog: Principles, Issues, and Decision Logic

### Change 1: Three-Tier Multiple-Mechanism Precedence Hierarchy
* **V2 Principle Preserved:** Physical reasoning over keyword matching; accurate identification of SIF precursors.
* **V2 Issue Addressed:** `ISSUE-V2-001` (P1 — Rule Precedence Collision).
* **Nature of Change:** **SPECIFICATION CHANGE**.
* **Description of Change:**
  * Added Section 2 (Principle 1) and Section 4 establishing a formal Three-Tier Precedence Hierarchy:
    * **Tier 1 (Catastrophic / High-Consequence):** Elevation falls, falling loads, live electrical contact, pressure rupture, deflagrations, confined spaces, heavy machine crush, vehicle collisions.
    * **Tier 2 (Intermediate):** Low-height descents ($<4$ ft), non-powered minor pinches, non-catastrophic thermal/pressure.
    * **Tier 3 (Low-Energy):** Manual tool slips, flat-ground slips/trips, office equipment, routine manual handling.
  * Enacted the **Precedence Rule**: when multiple mechanisms coexist in a narrative's event chain ($E_1 \rightarrow E_2 \rightarrow E_3$), the controlling mechanism is the highest tier present that directly caused worker exposure or injury.
* **Reason for Change:** In Phase 4B cases `PILOT_075` and `PILOT_076`, V2 matched "wrench slipped" under `LOW_ENERGY_MANUAL_TOOL` in Step 6 before evaluating a severe fall over a stairwell railing in Step 7, misclassifying catastrophic falls as `NO`.
* **Affected Decision Logic:** Low-energy mechanism matchers (Step 6) can no longer preempt high-energy hazard matchers (Step 7).
* **Future Implementation Required:** Yes (restructuring evaluation order and adding multi-mechanism hierarchy in engine).

### Change 2: Decoupling SIF Potential from Mandatory Barrier Failure
* **V2 Principle Preserved:** SIF precursor $\ne$ actual injury severity; barrier failure is not mandatory for every YES decision.
* **V2 Issue Addressed:** `ISSUE-V2-002` (P1 — Barrier Evidence Over-Coupling).
* **Nature of Change:** **SPECIFICATION CHANGE**.
* **Description of Change:**
  * Added Section 2 (Principle 2) explicitly defining the decision pathway:
    $$\text{HIGH\_CONSEQUENCE\_MECHANISM} \wedge \text{CREDIBLE\_HUMAN\_EXPOSURE} \wedge \text{SUFFICIENT\_EVIDENCE} \implies \text{SIF\_YES}$$
    even when $\text{BARRIER\_STATE} = \text{UNKNOWN}$.
  * Barrier status is formally classified as *supporting diagnostic evidence*, not an obligatory gatekeeper for `YES`.
* **Reason for Change:** In Phase 4B, 10 cases (e.g. `PILOT_010`, `PILOT_056`, `PILOT_057`, `PILOT_130`, `PILOT_131`) had unambiguous physical energy and direct exposure (e.g. fall from roof, fatal elevator pit fall), but V2 defaulted to `UNCERTAIN` solely because the narrative omitted mention of safety barriers.
* **Affected Decision Logic:** High-energy elevation falls and heavy machinery crush points with direct human exposure will evaluate to `YES` rather than falling through to `UNCERTAIN` when barriers are unmentioned.
* **Future Implementation Required:** Yes (updating Step 7 logic in engine to allow `YES` when `barrier_state == "UNKNOWN"` if energy and exposure are strong).

### Change 3: Generalized Semantic Equivalence Classes for Natural Phrasing
* **V2 Principle Preserved:** Narrative-only extraction; physical event mechanics over keyword exactness.
* **V2 Issue Addressed:** `ISSUE-V2-003` (P2 — Syntactic Coverage Narrowness).
* **Nature of Change:** **SPECIFICATION CHANGE**.
* **Description of Change:**
  * Added Section 2 (Principle 7) establishing three formal semantic equivalence classes:
    * **Class A (Elevation Falls):** Standardizes 11 verb phrases (`fell from`, `fell through`, `descended unintentionally`, `plunged from`, `tumbled from`, etc.) across 19 elevated structures (`roof`, `scaffold`, `ladder`, `catwalk`, `elevator shaft`, `stairwell`, `deck`).
    * **Class B (Mechanical Crush & Nip):** Standardizes 10 verb phrases (`drawn into`, `pulled into`, `entangled in`, `pinched by`, `compressed by`, `crushed by`) across 13 industrial machine types.
    * **Class C (Vehicle Line-of-Fire):** Standardizes 9 interaction verbs (`struck by`, `run over by`, `backed over by`, `pinned against`, `tipped over`) across 12 mobile plant classes.
* **Reason for Change:** V2 was heavily calibrated on 30 decision-table examples, causing natural OSHA abstract phrasings (like `PILOT_056` "fell through an unsecured section of railing and struck elevator pit floor") to miss regexes and default to `UNCERTAIN`.
* **Affected Decision Logic:** Broadens regex and NLP token matching to cover diverse OSHA linguistic patterns without relying on monolithic exact-string templates.
* **Future Implementation Required:** Yes (updating regex patterns in engine).

### Change 4: Explicit Medical-vs-Trauma Causal Stream Classification
* **V2 Principle Preserved:** SIF precursor is an occupational physical hazard; natural medical events are non-SIF.
* **V2 Issue Addressed:** `ISSUE-V2-004` (P2 — Medical-vs-Trauma Ambiguity).
* **Nature of Change:** **SPECIFICATION CHANGE**.
* **Description of Change:**
  * Added Section 2 (Principle 5) establishing three discrete causal classes:
    * `TRAUMA_DOMINANT`: Physical workplace energy directly caused harm $\rightarrow$ Evaluate SIF potential.
    * `MEDICAL_DOMINANT`: Spontaneous internal medical event without workplace trauma $\rightarrow$ `NO` (`NATURAL_MEDICAL_EVENT`).
    * `MIXED_UNCLEAR`: Co-occurrence of physical incident and delayed fatal medical event with ambiguous causality $\rightarrow$ **Strictly `UNCERTAIN`** (`INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION`) with medical review flag.
* **Reason for Change:** In `PILOT_084`, an employee was struck by a forklift backing out of a truck, but died at home 19 hours later from fluid in his lungs and a heart attack. V2 labeled it YES (forklift struck-by), while Reviewers labeled it NO (heart attack at home). Neither extreme is logically certain without medical autopsy causality.
* **Affected Decision Logic:** Introduces a dedicated `MIXED_UNCLEAR` causal check in Step 3 of the pipeline.
* **Future Implementation Required:** Yes (adding causal stream classifier in engine).

### Change 5: Two-Channel Architecture (Outcome Context Quarantine)
* **V2 Principle Preserved:** Actual injury severity does not dictate precursor potential; outcome decoupling.
* **V2 Issue Addressed:** `ISSUE-V2-005` (P2 — Outcome Bias Vulnerability).
* **Nature of Change:** **SPECIFICATION CHANGE**.
* **Description of Change:**
  * Added Section 2 (Principle 8) establishing two isolated channels:
    * **Precursor Evidence Channel:** Processes energy, exposure, barriers, and task kinematics. Exclusively drives SIF classification.
    * **Outcome Context Channel:** Ingests outcome keywords (death, fatal, amputation, fracture, surgery, hospitalization) strictly for reporting, explainability, and auditing.
  * Enacted the **Outcome Quarantine Rule**: Outcome context keywords are prohibited from voting on or upgrading a SIF decision.
* **Reason for Change:** Phase 4B identified 9 cases where severe outcome words (e.g. fractured ankle requiring surgery in `PILOT_004`) tempted high classification despite flat-ground low-energy mechanisms.
* **Affected Decision Logic:** Isolates token extractors into two distinct stages; outcome tokens cannot satisfy missing hazard energy requirements.
* **Future Implementation Required:** Yes (architectural separation of token extractors in engine).
